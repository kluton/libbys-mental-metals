Privacy Policy

Libby's Mental Metals · PAi Luton
Effective: August 13, 2026

Libby's Mental Metals is designed to perform its alloy analysis, relational modeling, validation planning, and speculative calculations locally on the user's Windows device.

Information we collect

PAi Luton does not operate analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry services inside Libby's Mental Metals. The application does not require an account and does not ask PAi Luton to collect names, email addresses, payment information, precise location, contacts, documents, or alloy inputs.

Local application data

Compositions, process parameters, calculated results, validation targets, and other information entered into the application are processed locally. Data saved or exported by the user remains under the user's control and is retained until the user changes or deletes it.

Network access

The desktop interface may communicate with services running locally on the same device. If a future version allows the user to enable an external model, database, website, or other third-party service, information intentionally sent to that service will be governed by the third party's privacy policy. PAi Luton does not control third-party data practices.

Sharing and sale of information

PAi Luton does not sell, rent, or share personal information collected through Libby's Mental Metals because the application does not transmit such information to PAi Luton.

Children's privacy

Libby's Mental Metals is a technical materials-analysis application and is not directed to children under 13. PAi Luton does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

Security

Local processing limits routine transmission of user data. Users remain responsible for securing their Windows account, device, exported files, backups, and any external services they choose to use.

Scientific and certification boundary

Privacy status does not change the application's scientific evidence labels. Predictions marked speculative, REQUIRES_DATA, or REQUIRES_VALIDATION are not certified material properties or qualified manufacturing instructions.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when application features or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Privacy questions may be submitted to PAi Luton through the support contact shown on the Microsoft Store listing for Libby's Mental Metals.
